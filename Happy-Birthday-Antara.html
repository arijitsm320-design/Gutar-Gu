Chapter IV

Our Gutar Gu

Since then, every day has been a scene from our own movie: soft, dreamy and full of love. We laugh, we grow, we hold on to each other.

Through every high and low, we remain each other's constant. This is not just a story. It is our Gutar Gu.

Chapter V

Moonlit talks

Somewhere between the first "hi" and the last "good night", the evenings became ours. We talked about everything and nothing: the silly teacher, the exam we feared, the dreams we only whispered to the dark.

I began to notice that time moved differently with you. Hours felt like minutes, and a quiet pause felt like a full conversation. That was when I knew my heart had already made its choice, long before my mind caught up.

Under the same moon, in different places, we were already sitting side by side.

"The moon was the only one who knew how much I smiled at your messages."
Chapter VI

Rain, and sorry

Every love story has its rainy chapters, and ours is no different. There were days of silence, misunderstandings, and words we wished we could take back. There were moments when pride stood between us and a simple "I miss you".

But the rain taught us something no sunny day could: that we choose each other even when it is hard. Every apology we made, every time one of us walked back first, quietly stitched our hearts tighter.

Now I know that a love worth keeping is one that learns to say sorry, and to say it softly.

"With you, even the storms feel like a place to belong."
Chapter VII

The art of waiting

There were days when life pulled us in different directions: exams, responsibilities, distance, and the quiet pressure of growing up. Some days I only had a few minutes to reach you, and I counted every one of them as treasure.

Waiting for your reply taught me patience. Waiting to see you taught me how much a single smile can be worth. And every time I did see you, the whole wait melted into one warm second.

I learned that love is not only the big moments. It is the small, steady ones: a message that says "reached home?", a voice note at midnight, a promise to call tomorrow. You made even the waiting beautiful.

Whatever the distance, my heart never learned any direction except yours.

"Distance is only a test of how loudly two hearts can speak."
Chapter VIII

Our own Vrindavan

Krishna has his flute and his peacock feather; Rai has the music that follows it. In our story you gave me my name, and I gave you yours. It became the most precious thing we share: a language only two people understand.

Rai was never just a name. She was the reason the flute played. And that is what you are to me: the reason every song in my life has a melody.

In our Vrindavan there are no crowds. There is only a quiet place where you call me Krishna, and I answer with all of my heart.

"Where there is love, there is always music, and where there is you, there is always home."

Chapter IX

Growing up, together

We are not the same two people who once sat on tuition benches. We have grown taller, wiser, a little more tired, and a lot more certain. And through every change, one thing stayed exactly where it started: us.

You cheered for my small wins when no one noticed them. I held your hand through the days that felt too heavy. We became each other's first call, our own safe corner of a very busy world.

Growing up with you taught me that love is not about finding someone perfect. It is about finding someone whose imperfections fit yours like the missing pieces of the same puzzle.

I would choose you in every version of life, every year, every birthday. Every single time.

"We grew like two branches of the same tree, different directions, same roots."
Chapter X

Toward forever

When I look ahead, I see a thousand quiet mornings with you, a thousand cups of chai, a thousand little arguments that end in laughter. I see us building something small and warm and ours.

I do not know exactly what the future holds. But I know who I want beside me while I find out. I want your hand in mine at every sunset, and your laugh as the soundtrack to every sunrise.

So here is my birthday promise to you, Sumana: I will keep walking with you, one gentle step at a time, until the road itself forgets where it was going.

"The sun may set a million times. I only need you to be there for every sunrise."

Words I never said

Things I should have told you sooner

I should have told you that the first time you laughed at my silly joke, I replayed that sound all night. I should have told you that I started carrying a little hope in my pocket every time I knew I would see you.

I should have told you that you make me braver than I am. With you I try harder, speak softer, dream bigger, and become someone I actually like. You never asked me to change, yet loving you has made me better in every quiet way.

I should have told you that on my worst days, the thought of you is the first light that comes in. And on my best days, you are the first person I want to tell. You are my beginning and my ending for every little story.

So I am telling you now, as loudly as a website can: I love you, Sumana. I love your mornings, your moods, your madness, your kindness. I love all of you, and I always will.
